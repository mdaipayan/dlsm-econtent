ADVANCED LEARNER Extension & Challenge Content · CO1–CO5

Beyond the Syllabus

Purpose

Open-ended, independent extension material for students who have mastered the basics — one section per Course Outcome, each with a real-world case study or field challenge and links to research-level resources, so you can go deeper than the core CO requires.

CO1

Design a Control Network

The core module covers what a control point is. This challenge asks you to design one from scratch.

Challenge
  1. Select an irregular plot (e.g. a section of your campus) and propose a control-point network for it, justifying the number and placement of points.
  2. Research and briefly compare two advanced digital surveying methods not covered in depth in class — LiDAR and UAV/drone photogrammetry — citing a credible source for each.
  3. Prepare a one-page technical note or poster presenting your proposed network with a sketch.
  4. Present your findings to the class in a five-minute peer-teaching session.
Extend further

Compare your proposed network's expected accuracy against a real cadastral or topographic survey specification for the same class of work.

Self-Check — CO1

Score: 0/3

CO2

Field Exercise: Live GPS Accuracy

Move from diagrams to a real receiver and quantify how good — or bad — standalone GPS actually is.

Challenge
  1. Using a handheld or smartphone GPS receiver, log the coordinates of the same point at three different times of day and compare the readings.
  2. Research Dilution of Precision (DOP) and how satellite geometry affects accuracy, supporting your explanation with data from the exercise.
  3. Investigate RTK-GPS and summarise one real infrastructure project that uses it.
  4. Submit a short technical report with recommendations for minimising the error you observed.

Self-Check — CO2

Score: 0/3

CO3

Case Study: DGPS/RTK in Practice

DGPS's real value shows up on large, real projects. Investigate one and extend the loop-closure concept to a bigger network.

Challenge
  1. Research a real infrastructure project (highway alignment, dam or port survey) that used DGPS/RTK and summarise the accuracy achieved and why it was chosen.
  2. Design a hypothetical closed-traverse control network of at least six stations and compute the theoretical loop-closure/misclosure adjustment.
  3. Compare the cost, accuracy and equipment requirements of DGPS versus static/RTK GNSS surveying.
  4. Present a five-minute case-study briefing to the class, citing at least one credible source.

Self-Check — CO3

Score: 0/3

CO4

Independent Field Survey & Contour Map

Take the Total Station from setup drills to a complete, independent survey deliverable.

Challenge
  1. Conduct an independent field survey of an open plot using the Total Station, recording horizontal angle, vertical angle and distance for at least 15 points.
  2. Process the recorded data and plot the points using survey/CAD software to generate contours.
  3. Identify and quantify at least one systematic error in your dataset (e.g. from instrument levelling) and propose a correction.
  4. Submit the finished contour map with a short methodology report.

Self-Check — CO4

Score: 0/3

CO5

Independent GIS Mapping Challenge

Go from a raw point dataset to a fully analysed, presentation-ready digital map.

Challenge
  1. Given a raw coordinate/point dataset with field codes, import and process it in QGIS or AutoCAD Civil 3D to generate a finished map with contours and boundaries.
  2. Add at least one thematic/analytical layer (e.g. land-use classification, slope analysis) using GIS tools.
  3. Research and briefly report on UAV/drone photogrammetry or LiDAR-based mapping as an extension to conventional software-based mapping.
  4. Present the finished map and a short methodology write-up to the class as a mini-project.

Self-Check — CO5

Score: 0/3
