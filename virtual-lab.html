Total Station Practical

Practise a complete first total-station practical before you go to the field: set up over the station, orient to a backsight, pick up details with codes, and set out a building. Every roll number gets its own site, so your readings are your own.

How it works. Type your roll number and press Start my practical. Work through the four stages in order; each stage opens when the previous one is complete. Your progress is saved in this browser. At the end, print your lab record (or save it as PDF) and submit it.

Tolerances used: centring 1 mm · levelling 30″ · backsight check 10 mm in distance and 20 mm in height · detail points 15 mm · pegs 15 mm. The instrument behaves like a ±(2 mm + 2 ppm), 2″ total station.
