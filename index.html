Course overview

Digital Land Surveying & Mapping introduces B.Tech Civil Engineering students to modern, instrument-based and satellite-based surveying methods that have replaced traditional chain-and-compass techniques, along with the software used to turn field measurements into finished maps.

Subject CodeBTCVE802T
ProgrammeB.Tech. (Civil Engineering) — Semester VIII
Credits3 (Internal: 30 Marks — 15 Sessional + 15 Activity · University: 70 Marks)
UnitsUnit I – Fundamentals of Surveying · Unit II – GPS · Unit III – DGPS · Unit IV – Total Station · Unit V – Mapping Software

Course outcomes

Each module below maps to one Course Outcome (CO) from the syllabus. Work through them in order, or jump straight to the topic you need.

CO1 · Unit I

Fundamentals of Digital Land Surveying

Types of surveying, the digital surveying concept, control points, and modern data-capture methods such as LiDAR and UAV photogrammetry.

Explore module CO2 · Unit II

Global Positioning System (GPS)

Space, control and user segments, signal structure, receiver types, and the sources of GPS positioning error.

Explore module CO3 · Unit III

Differential GPS (DGPS)

The differential correction principle, base-and-rover working, control station setup, loop closure and accuracy gains over standalone GPS.

Explore module CO4 · Unit IV

Total Station Surveying

Instrument parts, setting-up procedure, angle and distance measurement, contouring, and common sources of error.

Explore module CO5 · Unit V

Mapping Fundamentals & Software

Scale, projections and accuracy standards, plus how CAD/GIS mapping software turns survey data into finished digital maps.
